Nam: văn bản quy phạm pháp luật, văn bản hành chính, và tin thời sự thuần túy đưa tin về các văn bản đó. Điều 15 Luật

     văn bản đó.

State documents and news reports in the public data source snapshots

The files that REUSE.toml declares with this identifier are under seed/du-lieu-mo/. They are copies of, or quotes from,
Vietnamese state documents: legal normative documents, administrative documents, and pure news reports about those
documents. Article 15 of the Law on Intellectual Property (consolidated text 160/2026/VBHN-LQ-VPQH) states that the
following are not protected by copyright:

  1. News of current events that is purely informational.
  2. Legal normative documents, administrative documents, other documents in the judicial field, and their official
     translations.
  3. Procedures, systems, methods of operation, concepts, principles and data.

Signet claims no copyright in these files and does not relicense them under Apache-2.0. The source of each file (URL,
fetch date, SHA-256, reuse basis) is listed in the manifest.json of the same folder. When you reuse them, credit the
source as the original page asks. The fetch code (scripts/) and the files it generates (manifest.json, normalised data)
remain under Apache-2.0.
